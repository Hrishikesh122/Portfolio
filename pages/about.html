About

Computer science student, currently splitting my time between machine learning research and security work.

I am Hrishikesh Harnoor, a B.Tech Computer Science student at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Amaravati. Most of what I do falls into two buckets that keep turning out to be the same bucket: building machine learning systems, and understanding how systems fail under an adversary.

On the research side that has meant unsupervised intrusion detection for IoT networks, published at IEEE IECS 2026, and multimodal malware detection that reasons across memory forensics, network traffic and binary imagery at once. On the security side it has meant writing RSA attack tooling, implementing a hybrid post-quantum key exchange with a working downgrade attack against it, and generating real Groth16 zero-knowledge proofs rather than mocking them.

I like problems where a wrong answer is expensive and the failure mode is not obvious. If you are working on something in that shape, I would like to hear about it.
